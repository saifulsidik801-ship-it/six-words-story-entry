One complete story in exactly six words — the entry for make.fun's "Six Words, One Story" bounty ($5 in USDC, top three take the pool).

The story:

> **His lunchbox came home. He didn't.**

A before (a child leaves with his lunchbox) and an after (the lunchbox returns, the child does not) in two short sentences. The object coming home while the person does not is the turn: a whole life — school mornings, a packed lunch, a family waiting — and a loss, without a single word of it spelled out. `main.ts` holds eleven candidate stories, scores each against the judge's published brief (complete story first, then a turn, then every word earning its place, then clean rhythm, then something left unsaid), and writes the winner to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```

The program needs no dependencies and no network. It prints the ranked candidates, then writes `entry.txt` (34 characters, exactly six words).
